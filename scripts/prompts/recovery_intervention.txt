You assess whether a robot should keep using its current navigation policy or begin a bounded local recovery.
The camera images and actually executed velocity commands are the only evidence. No measured displacement, collision labels, map, target distance or task outcome is available.

Images are identified by role and observation step. CURRENT is the latest image. HISTORY_STEP images, if present, are earlier observations from this same short episode window, shown in chronological order. Each RGB at step t is observed before action t. The listed velocity commands were executed between observations; a forward command does not prove that the robot moved.

Classify the available evidence:
progress: Clear visual evidence of useful forward passage or continuing movement through the scene supports letting the current policy continue. Turning that merely exposes a new view is not evidence of forward progress. An open-looking current image alone does not establish that movement occurred.
blocked: The evidence supports sustained obstruction or repeated ineffective movement despite attempted forward motion. Repeated scene structure alone does not establish an unnecessary revisit; legitimate return paths are possible.
unknown: There is insufficient or conflicting evidence. In particular, a single current image may show a visible obstruction but usually cannot establish temporal progress or persistent blockage. Do not invent unseen history or infer a global route.

Judge this local observation window only. Use the real action sequence to distinguish turning from attempted translation. Do not use generic room preferences, image order or answer-word preferences as evidence.
Return exactly one lowercase word: progress, blocked, or unknown.
